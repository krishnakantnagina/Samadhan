1. Samadhan lets any citizen of Madhya Pradesh file a civic complaint by speaking or typing in their own Hindi, Hinglish or local dialect, with no forms and no need to know which department to approach.
2. Our main goal: language and dialect must never be a barrier between a citizen and their government.
3. We do not need perfect transcription of every dialect. We only need to understand the complaint well enough to route it to the right department, and to ask for what is missing.
4. The AI proposes and plain code verifies: every value is checked against a service specification, so the system cannot invent a department, field or office, and unsure cases go to a human review queue.
5. The citizen gets a ticket number (SMD-xxxx) and can check status by voice or text. Officers get a ready-classified ticket on a dashboard with a review queue, reassignment and a map.
6. It works today as a live prototype with five services, about 90% routing accuracy on 60 test messages, and 415 + 35 automated tests passing. We state our limits openly: demo office data and synthetic dialect test data.
7. Next goal: verified ward and office data, phone verification by SMS OTP once we obtain the SMS licence, and rate limiting, so it is safe to open to the public.
8. Then more channels on the same engine: WhatsApp and phone calls, so citizens without a smartphone or internet can simply speak to Samadhan.
9. Then per-department officer accounts and more departments and districts across Madhya Pradesh: adding a department is a specification file, not a rewrite.
10. Samadhan is a self-service channel that complements MPOnline's platform and can reduce call-centre load: a voice for every citizen, understood in their own words.
